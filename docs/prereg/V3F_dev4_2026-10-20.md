## Deviation (2026-10-20): V3F deviation 4, the data build's keys and the verdict decided before its arms existed

Made on 2026-10-20 by the delegate under the 2026-09-30 owner grant (V3F.10), after the family's data build (L23H) had failed twice on 2026-10-12 and the family's verdict had been decided on 2026-10-13 while eight arms had never started. Family numbers existed (listed below), so each change is a deviation under V3F.3.9, listed in the family report with those numbers. None changes a pre-registered rule, arm, exam, key list, manifest, count, threshold, Holm set or read order: each makes the platform do what the amendment already says. The amendment's text (sha256 5826660c…2f7f), Revisions 29-31 (36f46378…f814) and deviations 1-3 (`docs/prereg/V3F_dev1-3_2026-10-12.md`) are unchanged. **What happened.** Both data builds (jobs 49061040 and 49062364, 2026-10-12, 1,794 s and 530 s) stopped in their first DINOv2 scan with `funnel.embed.EmbedError: embed_images: duplicate keys`. The ticker recorded L23H failed after its second failure, recorded the builds and D6 "not run (the family's data build failed)" as ended, ran the remaining scores, and proposed L23L. Job 49107774 (2026-10-13, 12:08-12:47Z) decided the verdict (`capacity/v3fam_v1.json`, sha256 304bad59…c792b) with BCLEAN, D1, D4F, D4N, D4R15, D5B, D6 and D8 missing. V3F.7 step 11 runs the verdict "once every arm's record is complete or failed"; those eight arms had no record and had never started. The build's partial outputs stayed on the cluster: `splits/v3fam/d5b/` (476 copy PNGs and 476 labels, no manifest, no summary) and `v3fam/v1/leak/ood_desc.npz`.
