#### V3F.3.2 The statistics and the one scoring path

- *Species score*: the locked scorer's `species_map50_95` (Ultralytics 8.4.37 at 640, conf 0.001, IoU 0.7, scorer sha256 18c00837…), the mean AP50-95 over the species with a GT box in the exam, OtherPlant left out (`tools/inc/scorer.py:464`). On ImageWeeds it is Ragweed AP alone (V3F.2).
- *Agnostic score*: the class-collapsed AP50-95 of the rows after `one_per_box`, `inc.scorer.collapsed_ap`, as E1 computed it (CLOOP:2901). For a box detector on ooddev_v1 and on the stage-1 readings it is the detector's own box set.
- *Target-species agnostic AP with ignore regions* (P7 only, decision 7): GT = every non-OtherPlant box; prediction rows (after `one_per_box`) are matched to it class-collapsed by Ultralytics' `match_predictions` at IoU 0.50:0.95, as E3's stage-1 helper matches (`twostage.py` `e3_stage1`); a prediction that is not a true positive at IoU 0.50 and whose IoU with some OtherPlant GT box is ≥ 0.5 is removed from every threshold (neither TP nor FP; an OtherPlant box may absorb any number of predictions); AP50-95 = `collapsed_ap` on the remaining rows with n_gt = the target GT count (4,139 on ood23). The symmetric reading (OtherPlant GT, target boxes as ignore regions) is OtherPlant's separate number. A hand-computed case pins both (V3F.6.5).
- *One path for both sides (decision 17).* Every score of every comparison — the comparators E3-M, E3-A, E3-B and b_v2_m640 included — is taken through the family pass `v3fam._family_pass`: one `inc.scorer.score` call with the family validator installed. The family validator is a subclass of E3's own validator class (`twostage.e3_validator_class(cfg)[0]`, itself built on the scorer sidecar's capturing validator, built on the locked scorer's), imported, never edited. Its modes are E3's identity, two_stage and gt, plus: `stage1` (a one-class detector's box set and its class-collapsed matches, the P7 statistic), `inject_boxes` (D3: a given box set per image replaces stage 1, then E3's emission), `inject_rows` (D6: given rows in original pixels replace the predictions after NMS), `oracle` (O5: a stage-1 box with IoU ≥ 0.5 to a GT box is emitted once with that GT class at q; any other box takes E3's top-3) and `view640` (D2's BioCLIP-2@640 crops). Emission, limits (conf 0.001, max_det 300), geometry (per-axis inverse letterbox), the GT round trip (1e-3) and the size check are E3's (CLOOP:3786-3813). Per-image arrays come from `scorer_sidecar.check_capture`/`flatten`.
- *EXIF.* A pass reads EXIF tags none, 0 and 1 (`twostage.OK_ORIENTATIONS`, imported) on every exam, and also tags 3, 6 and 8 (`twostage.FIT_ORIENTATIONS`, which adds them to the first three) on ood22 and ood23 only, once the frame pre-flight has passed: on one base_v2 copy each of tags 3, 6 and 8 (and one of tag 0), the array Ultralytics' validator loads equals PIL `exif_transpose`'s within a mean absolute difference of 2 grey levels, its shape equals `ori_shape`, and the GT boxes drawn on it lie on the plants. ood23's images carry 606 tags 3/6/8 (156 + 401 + 49) and 54 tag 0 (read from their base_v2 copies, F1; CLOOP:3796); ood22's carry none (1,912 none, 3 tag 1).

#### V3F.3.3 The decision rule, its corrections and the outcome classes

For a comparison "X over Y" on one exam, with seeds 0, 1, 2 on each side:
- D = mean(X) − mean(Y). X **qualifies** when D > 2 × pooled sd (√((sd_X² + sd_Y²)/2), sample sd) **and** D > SE(D) — the E-rule, unchanged (CLOOP:3818-3824).
- *Image SE*: the paired image bootstrap, 1,000 resamples of the exam's images, one draw for every run, the statistic on each run's tie-broken per-image arrays, mean over seeds per side, X − Y, SE = the sample sd over resamples. The family loop is built from `scorer_sidecar`'s `resample_indices`, `tie_break`, `species_arrays` and `class_ap` (imported) and must reproduce `inc2.baseline.native_bootstrap`'s SE exactly on the same arrays and seed text (a test); it exists because it also returns each resample's value and accepts session draws. Seed text `stable_int("inc2/v3fam/<comparison id>/image")`.
- *Session-cluster SE* (decision 6): 1,000 resamples of the exam's sessions with replacement, every image of a drawn session taken with its session's multiplicity, the same statistic; seed text `…/<comparison id>/session`. For P6–P9 the SE used in the rule and in z is the **larger** of the image SE and the session SE. On dev it is reported beside every dev comparison (8 sessions), never deciding.
- *Selection correction for P2, P3 and P5* (decision 5): in each image resample r, E3's choice is redone among E3-M, E3-A and E3-B from their family-path dev arrays: c(r) = the arm with the largest resample species mean over seeds (ties M, then A, then B, E3's tie order; the qualification conditions are not re-evaluated in a resample, since all three qualified on the full set). Then T_r = S*_{c(r)} − S*_Y, where Y is the comparison's other side (TS-F, TS-N or RF-DETR-L; E3-B is X in P2, P3 and P5). The selection-adjusted difference is D_sel = D − mean_r[T_r − (S_{c(r)} − S_Y)] (S without a star: the full-set value) and SE_sel = sd_r(T_r). The rule and z for P2, P3 and P5 use D_sel and SE_sel; the plain D and SE and the share of resamples in which B is chosen are reported beside. P1 and P4 compare systems on E3-B's own boxes, so the selection of the box set is shared by both sides and is not corrected (stated).
- *Exceptions* (declared with each experiment): P4's ensemble is one system, so its 2-sd condition is D > 2 × sd(E3-B) and its seed term (2 sd(E3-B)²)/3; P1 and every D2 comparison are paired on boxes (the unpaired pooled sd is conservative, as for E3-M, CLOOP:3824; paired per-seed differences reported beside).
- *Combined z*: z = D / σ_c with σ_c = √(SE² + (sd_X² + sd_Y²)/3), the image-sampling variance plus the seed variance of a difference of two 3-seed means. The reference is normal. The seed term has about 4 degrees of freedom, so the normal reference is slightly anti-conservative for it (one-sided 5 %: t₄ 2.132 vs 1.645); in every recorded E verdict the bootstrap term dominates (E3-B: SE² 7.2e-5 against a seed term of 2.7e-6, derived), and this is stated in the paper (O7).
- *SESOI* (decision 4) = 0.005 mAP50-95 on dev. The same 0.005 applies to the ood, probe and ooddev exams (one number for one unit, extended by the delegate, not tuned per exam).
- *Outcome classes of a primary*, mutually exclusive, in this order, each with that primary's own D, SE and seed term (D_sel and SE_sel for P2, P3 and P5; the larger SE for P6–P9; P4's one-system seed term):
  1. **qualifies** (the rule above). It also carries the flag "below SESOI" when its 90 % interval D ± 1.645 σ_c lies inside (−0.005, +0.005);
  2. **equivalent**: it does not qualify and its 90 % interval lies inside (−0.005, +0.005);
  3. **inconclusive**: neither. It carries the flag "reversed" when the whole 90 % interval lies below 0.
- *Nothing switches.* A qualifying arm changes no stream arm, pool, incumbent or deployed model. Every family record is record-only, as in E1–E3.

**Holm overlay per exam** (decision 3). A primary is **family-confirmed** when it qualifies and its z survives Holm's step-down at one-sided α = 0.05 within its exam: dev {P1–P5} (m = 5: z > 2.326, 2.241, 2.128, 1.960, 1.645 in order of z, stopping at the first failure), ood22 {P6} and ood23 {P7} (m = 1: z > 1.645), probe {P8, P9} (m = 2: 1.960, then 1.645). Across the four exam families the family-wise error can reach 1 − 0.95⁴ = 0.185 (independent families) and at most 0.20; the paper states this convention and number (O6). A primary whose arm was not run or failed (a failed download, D6's walltime refusal, a job that failed twice, a refusal at the family cap of decision 25) keeps its place in its exam's Holm set, so m does not shrink; it is reported "not run" and is never confirmed. Worked check on recorded values (derived): E1 B over A z = 3.06, E3-B 3.87, E3-M 2.92, E3-A 1.43, E3's attribution 2.97; taken as one dev set of five, Holm would confirm all but E3-A (1.43 < 1.645).

Only family-confirmed primaries, and primaries whose outcome is "equivalent" (stated with "within ±0.005, 90 % interval"), may appear as findings in the Abstract, Highlights and Conclusions. Everything else is labelled exploratory.

**Equivalence is per primary** (decision 28). "Equivalent" is judged on each primary's own 90 % interval, which is a two one-sided tests (TOST) procedure at 5 % for that primary; Holm applies to qualification only, never to equivalence, and no other multiplicity correction is applied to equivalence claims. If every primary's true difference sat at the ±0.005 margin, the chance that at least one is called equivalent could reach 1 − 0.95⁹ = 0.370 for independent primaries and at most 0.45 (9 × 0.05, derived; W2). The paper states this convention and these numbers wherever an equivalence claim appears, and in the Methods.

For each, D = challenger − E3-B on dev species (plain D: the selection bias favours E3-B, so the gate is conservative). A challenger opens a test read only when it qualifies over E3-B **and** its z > Φ⁻¹(1 − 0.05/14) = 2.6901 (2.690110 to six decimals; W2). At most 3 challenger reads, largest D first (2 if D0-S's conditional re-run takes a read, V3F.3.6).

**Tier S: secondary (exploratory).** Every other comparison named in V3F.5 is computed the same way and reported; none opens a read or a claim.

#### D0-S — residual-copy sweep aimed at the detector's blind spots (O1; decision 1)

- *Question.* Does base B hold crop or letterbox copies of dev or test images that dHash and the calibrated detector miss (calibrated recall: crops 0.7185, letterbox-640 0.30, `splits/v2/embed_calibration_v2.json`)? The exposure is asymmetric: E1-B adds 37,674 external rows, E1-A 66.
- *Sets.* Query Q = dev ∪ test (2,594 images). Reference R = base B's 37,674 external rows plus base_v2's 66 NDSU rows (37,740), by key from the base B manifest. Pixels only; no label or score is read.
- *Procedure (fixed now).*
  1. Letterbox trim on both sets: from each side, strip rows (columns) whose pixels all lie within 8 grey levels of that side's median border colour and whose standard deviation is under 2, moving inward until one fails.
  2. Descriptor: DINOv2-base CLS under the guard's calibrated view (LOCK v2's detector). Query views: the whole image, its horizontal flip, centre crops 0.9 and 0.8, and the four 0.8 corner crops (8 views, 20,752 in all). Reference: the trimmed whole image; a row with no trimmed border reuses base v3's stored descriptor (`splits/v3/leak/scan_desc.npz`, 86,563 × 768 fp16, keyed by row uid; F1) if 100 seeded rows recomputed give cosine ≥ 0.999 to it, else every row is recomputed.
  3. Shortlist: for each query view, the 5 nearest reference rows by cosine, kept when cosine ≥ 0.70; plus every pair within 10 bits under the 8-variant dHash in both directions on the trimmed images.
  4. Verification: OpenCV SIFT (4,000 features) on greyscale images resized to long side 1,024, Lowe ratio 0.8, RANSAC homography (reprojection 5 px, 2,000 iterations, confidence 0.995), each pair also with the reference flipped; **verified** at ≥ 50 inliers.
  5. Calibration, recorded and never used to move a threshold: five copy families (centre crop 0.9, centre crop 0.8, letterbox to 640 with grey padding, JPEG quality 75, horizontal flip) of 200 dev ∪ test images drawn by `stable_int("inc2/v3fam/sweep/calib/<key>")`, placed in a scratch reference index (never a training list), and 200 seeded non-copy pairs; recall per family and false verifications are reported.
  6. Visual verdict on every verified pair (side-by-side montage with cosine and inliers), by a named viewer ("Harry He (owner)" or "Claude (delegate under the 2026-09-30 owner grant)"). The verdict is "copy" when the two images show the same photograph (the same scene, plants and capture) up to cropping, resizing, padding, flipping, rotation or re-encoding. It is "no" otherwise, which includes two photographs of the same plant or field taken at different moments.
  A **true copy** is a verified pair whose verdict is "copy". H = the query images with a true copy.
- *Trigger (pre-registered).* The first two branches apply together when H holds both test and dev images.
  - H ∩ test ≠ ∅:
    - (a) the re-analysis job scores E3-A, E3-M, E3-B and the reference on test∖H from their stored per-image arrays;
    - (b) **BCLEAN** runs: E1-B's manifest minus the copies' rows, one-class m640 at `cold_budget`, 3 seeds (L23I `D0BCLEAN`, experiment `v3_d0_bclean_m640`), scored as a two-stage arm with E3's pinned classifier (L23J `BCLEAN`). It is judged by the E3 rule against b_v2_m640 on dev (seed text `inc2/v3fam/bclean/species_se`), read against E3-B as a Tier S reading, and given one test read inside the cap. BCLEAN's build is the first item of step 8 and its scoring the first item of step 10 (V3F.7);
    - (c) the copies leave every family training set (V3F.3.7 item 4).
  - H ∩ dev ≠ ∅:
    - every family dev comparison (P1–P5, the Tier C gate, Tier S) is decided on dev∖H, the same images for every arm, with the image and session bootstraps and the selection correction run on dev∖H. One side of P2, P3 and P5 (E3-B, through E1-B) would otherwise have trained on copies of exam images that the other side's training set no longer holds;
    - the full-dev numbers are reported beside;
    - E3's recorded dev comparisons are re-read on dev∖H from E3RS's arrays and reported only;
    - the copies leave every family training set.
  - H = ∅: the record says so; the paper states the sweep's yield and its calibrated recall.
- *Bound (O8, descriptive).* The sweep's residual copy rate (|H ∩ test| / 1,977, or its calibrated upper bound when H = ∅) times D5b's inflation per copied image bounds the inflation the blind spots could cause in the headline.
- *Outputs.* `v3fam/v1/sweep/record.json` (written once: sets and their sha256, counts per step, pairs, inliers, verdicts and viewers, calibration, H) and the montages. L23H is not proposed before the record is complete.
